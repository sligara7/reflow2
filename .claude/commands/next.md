Call reflow2's `what_next` tool and read its answer back to me. **The ranking is the tool's, not yours** — do not substitute your own sense of what matters, and do not reorder what it returns. If you think it has missed something, say so as a separate remark after the bands.

Keep the bands apart, because they answer different questions:

- **What I marked** — decisions carrying my own approver edge. This is my word, it survives every session, and no score reorders it. Lead with it.
- **The ranked few** — the highest-scoring decisions I have *not* marked. Say *why* each one surfaced ("five things wait on it", "it contradicts something settled"), never the bare number.
- **The one unexplored** — say plainly that it is a deliberate sample of the decisions nothing points at yet, **not the least important one**. Scoring zero means the graph has no opinion, never that the question does not matter.

**Say what is not shown.** Report `not_shown` and `unranked_pool` so a five-item answer never reads as the whole set. **And say the ranking is rough** — the score is coarse and its head is usually a near-tie, so offer it as a starting point rather than an ordering. A Decision carries no timestamp, so nothing about age enters the score at all.

Skip the `shaping` band unless I ask what shapes the design, or I am new to it — those are *settled* decisions that need nothing from anybody, and they are orientation rather than news.

Plain language, no node ids. If nothing is open, say so plainly instead of padding.
