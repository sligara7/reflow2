Reflow 2.0 — projected viewpoints

Every element below is projected from reflow2.json (715 nodes, 2263 edges). Nothing is hand-drawn: the renderer may only emit what the graph states, and everything it needed but could not find is confessed at the end — per the doctrine that a fill-in during rendering is a defect in the model, not a job for the agent. The catalogue: docs/viewpoints.md.
