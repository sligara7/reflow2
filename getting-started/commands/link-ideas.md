description: Join the half-ideas — find which recorded ideas belong together, and link them
---
Use the **link-ideas** skill to work this project's backlog of ideas that connect to nothing.

Take a handful, not the whole list — five to ten in a sitting. Prefer ones near what we have been talking about, since those are the ones whose relations can actually be judged.

For each one, ask the graph what might relate to it, then read both nodes yourself before deciding. Put the real candidates to me in plain language: what the two ideas are, and what you think the relation is — does one depend on the other, contradict it, retire it, or did the older one see the newer coming?

- Never draw an edge to make the count go down. A false neighbour is worse than a missing one, because everything that searches by neighbourhood repeats it forever.
- If nothing is honestly related, say so and record that as the answer, naming what you looked at and what was nearest. That is a real result, not a failure.
- Tell me at the end which ideas you worked, what you linked, and what you recorded as genuinely unrelated. Then stop.
